1. Extract latest Life Lessons version to your Rimworld Mods folder (https://gitlab.com/Ghost.Data/life-lessons/-/releases/v2.0.17).
2. Open the Life Lessons folder you have placed in your Rimworld Mods folder, only delete these: "Content", "Defs", "Languages", "Patches", "LoadFolders".
3. You can now play. This can be added or updated mid-save and works when tested. Unpatched mods do not have any issues, however they will be buildable, craftable etc. without any proficiency required like vanilla behaviour.
